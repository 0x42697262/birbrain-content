Protection of computer systems or networks from xref:cyberattack.adoc[Cyberattack]s by xref:threat-actor.adoc[Threat Actor]s.
It is a practice of ensuring xref:cia-triad.adoc[confidentiality, integrity, and availability] by protecting networks, devices, people, and data.
Unlike xref:information-security.adoc[Information Security] that establish security measures to keep information safe.
