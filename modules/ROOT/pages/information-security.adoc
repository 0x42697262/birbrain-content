Establish security measures to keep information safe.
Unlike xref:computer-security.adoc[Computer Security] that focuses on protecting information from xref:cyberattack.adoc[Cyberattack]s.

Basic tenets:

* Confidentiality
* Integrity
* Availability

or also known as xref:cia-triad.adoc[CIA Triad] which is its primary focus while maintaining efficiency without hampering productivity.

It is important to satisfy the 8 domains of xref:security-domains-cissp.adoc[Security Domains (CISSP)].
