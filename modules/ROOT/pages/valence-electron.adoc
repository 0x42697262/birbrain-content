== What is a Valence Electron

Valence Electrons are *s* and *p* xref:electron.adoc[electrons] located at the outermost shell of an xref:atom.adoc[atom].

* max number of xref:electron.adoc[electrons] in an xref:atom.adoc[atom] is 8
