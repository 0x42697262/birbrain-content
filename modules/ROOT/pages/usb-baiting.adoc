= USB Baiting
:page-origin: Obsidian
:page-date: 2024-03-11
:page-tags: social-engineering

// Imported from `Development/obsidian_files/» Notes/Atomic/USB Baiting.md`.

Intentionally planting malicious USB flash drives at designated place as a form of xref:social-engineering.adoc[Social Engineering] attack by the xref:threat-actor.adoc[Threat Actor]s to an individual or group.
Inside the USB flash drive might contain Malware such as Keyloggers, Remote Access Trojans, or Ransomwares.

Examples:

* USB Killer
* USB Rubber Ducky
