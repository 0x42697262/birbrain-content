Nucleosynthesis is the study of nuclear processes responsible for the formation of the xref:element.adoc[elements].

* basically *creation* of new elements
* heavier elements are created through fusion of lighter elements
* process that creates new atomic nuclei from preexisting nucleons and nuclei
