Process of converting xref:plain-text.adoc[Plain Text] into xref:cipher-text.adoc[Cipher Text].
To convert information into cipher text, plain text information must be run under an Encryption Algorithm.
Which is similar method for decryption.

Most common types of xref:cipher.adoc[Cipher] are xref:substitution-cipher.adoc[Substitution Cipher] and xref:transposition-cipher.adoc[Transposition Cipher].
